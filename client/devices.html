My Collection

Your collection of devices is your identity on {{server.domain}}

Your collection of devices is you. Your phone, your tablet, your home PC, your work PC — each one is a member of your collection. Together they share your keys, share your content, and protect each other.

Each browser is treated as its own device — even when two browsers run on the same machine. Chrome on your PC and Edge on the same PC are two separate devices. Add as many as you use.

The power of your collection is what makes your content easy to use, easy to manage, and easy to recover.

Examples of devices in a collection:

🌐 Chrome on your home PC 🌐 Edge on your home PC 🌐 Chrome at work 📱 Safari on your iPhone 📋 Tablet browser 🛡️ Epistery recovery

Your content — your pictures, your conversations, your notes — is encrypted with keys that only your collection holds. No one else can read it. Not even {{server.domain}}.

If a device is lost or stolen, remove its connection here. It can never access your content again, and everything is still safe on your other connections.

Epistery is also in your collection — as a recovery service, not as an owner. If you're ever down to your last connection, Epistery can help you get back in. Remove it at any time and the service immediately loses all access. Zero.

Epistery in your collection

{{server.domain}} joins your collection as a recovery service — one member among your devices, not a special owner. It uses this access only to help you recover if you lose all your other devices.

You are always in control. Remove Epistery from your collection at any time using the Remove button above. The moment you do, the service has no access to your keys, your data, or your account — now or ever. You can add it back whenever you choose.

Think of it like a trusted locksmith who holds one key to your house. You gave them the key — you can take it back. They can't enter without it.
